ABOUT MUBA Blockchain Hackathon

A hackathon to ship Malaysia's next blockchain builders.

MUBA Blockchain Hackathon 2026 is Malaysia's first large-scale student-led hackathon focused entirely on Web3, bringing together developers, designers, and founders across Malaysia to build, ship, and demo across 11 days online.

Hackers form teams, choose a sponsor track, build against real APIs and SDKs, and pitch live to track-specific judges nominated by each sponsor.
