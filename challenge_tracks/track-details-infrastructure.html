Infrastructure: Web3 Foundations

Welcome to the Infrastructure track. In this track, participants are challenged to build tools, protocols, and foundational layers to scale the Web3 ecosystem.

Detailed Requirements

  • Must provide a functional prototype or developer tooling package.
  • Innovative scalability solutions are highly encouraged.
  • Solutions should handle high throughput and low latency.

Judging Criteria Deep Dive

Projects will be heavily evaluated on technical architecture and code security. Make sure to provide a robust test suite and documentation.

Ready to submit?

Ensure your GitHub repository is public and all presentation materials are uploaded to the hackathon portal before the deadline.
