Consumer Apps: Mass Adoption

Welcome to the Consumer Apps track. In this track, participants are challenged to create engaging decentralized applications tailored for everyday users.

Detailed Requirements

  • Must be deployed with a fully functional frontend interface.
  • Innovative user experience (UX) designs are highly encouraged.
  • Solutions should abstract away blockchain complexities for the end user.

Judging Criteria Deep Dive

Projects will be heavily evaluated on design aesthetics and usability. Make sure your application is responsive and accessible.

Ready to submit?

Ensure your GitHub repository is public and all presentation materials are uploaded to the hackathon portal before the deadline.
