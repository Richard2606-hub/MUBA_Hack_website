Stablecoins: Next-Gen Stability

Welcome to the Stablecoins track. In this track, participants are challenged to build stable and reliable cryptocurrency solutions pegged to real-world assets or algorithmic models.

Detailed Requirements

  • Must be deployed on a testnet with verifiable smart contracts.
  • Innovative pegging mechanisms are highly encouraged.
  • Solutions should handle high volatility scenarios gracefully.

Judging Criteria Deep Dive

Projects will be heavily evaluated on economic sustainability and code security. Make sure to provide a robust test suite for your smart contracts.

Ready to submit?

Ensure your GitHub repository is public and all presentation materials are uploaded to the hackathon portal before the deadline.
